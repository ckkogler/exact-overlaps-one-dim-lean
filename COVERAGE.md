# Mathematical coverage

The primary source is Samuel Kittle and Constantin Kogler, *The exact overlaps conjecture for self-similar measures on the real line*. The selected scope consists of its seventeen numbered results, Definition 3.5, their necessary inputs, and Michael Hochman's Theorem 1.4. Bibliographic attribution is in [provenance/BIBLIOGRAPHY.md](provenance/BIBLIOGRAPHY.md).

[Challenge.lean](Challenge.lean) states two main theorems and a corollary from the introduction: Theorem 1.1, Corollary 1.2 and Theorem 1.3. Two necessary entropy preliminaries prove subadditivity and identify the random-walk entropy-rate infimum with the ordinary limit. Three elementary normalization/support lemmas make the actual probability laws well defined. All eight Challenge theorem declarations are compared with Solution. Later results belong to the proved library. Names below start with `ExactOverlaps.`.

## Meanings and inherited hypotheses

The system stores actual nonzero affine maps, strict absolute contraction and nonnegative probability weights. The main dimension theorem allows zero listed weights, unequal contraction ratios, reflections and exact overlaps. Corollary 1.2 alone imposes absence of exact overlaps. The library proves exact dimensionality and identifies its exponent with the standard lower Hausdorff dimension used in the public statements.

Finite entropy uses natural logarithms and the convention `0 log 0=0`. The random-walk law identifies coincident actual affine maps by their translation and signed ratio; it is not the free word law. Subadditivity and the entropy-rate limit are proved. Physical scale entropy averages the actual floor-quantized law over translations. The countable version used for unbounded laws agrees with the bounded-support version, with finiteness and integrability proved before real-valued use.

Variance energy is first defined by extended nonnegative integrals, with normalization `4/r³` and scale measure `dr/r`. General bounded laws may have infinite energy. Every real conversion needed for finite laws is supported by a finiteness proof. Empty conditional windows and zero-probability fibers are treated explicitly.

The Poisson arguments use the exact independent cut indicators in gaps between successive positive-mass support points. This finite observable representation includes the partition in the observation and preserves every quantity in Lemma 2.3 and Theorem 1.3. It does not claim to construct an unrelated full planar point process.

Hochman's input is proved independently of the primary main dimension formula. Its dependencies include the sufficient inverse entropy statement, Gaussian approximation, component estimates, exact dimensionality, signed ratio conditioning and scale comparison. Necessary existence or comparison statements are proved rather than stored as assumptions in the system type.

## Exclusions and proof changes

Informal remarks, examples, historical discussion, speculation, introductory proof sketches and unrelated applications of Hochman's paper are not separate targets. The remark about an optimal Gaussian approximation constant is excluded; Lemma 3.7 asks for an absolute positive constant. Necessary intermediate claims are still proved when a selected result uses them.
